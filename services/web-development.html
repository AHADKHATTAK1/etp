Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a website take to build?
A standard custom website typically takes 4-6 weeks from discovery to launch. Complex web apps or e-commerce platforms can take 8-12 weeks.
Do you develop WordPress websites?
Yes, we build highly optimized, custom WordPress websites that are easy for you to manage, utilizing modern tech like headless setups when necessary.
React vs WordPress: Which is right for me?
It depends on your needs. Next.js/React is perfect for high-performance web apps, complex integrations, and large-scale platforms. WordPress is great for content-heavy marketing sites.
Will my website be fast and responsive?
Absolutely. All our websites are built mobile-first and optimized rigorously for Core Web Vitals, ensuring lightning-fast load times on all devices.
What's included in ongoing support?
Our support packages include security updates, daily backups, performance monitoring, technical SEO checks, and dedicated hours for content or feature updates.
