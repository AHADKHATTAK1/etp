Frequently Asked Questions

How long does SEO take?
SEO is a long-term strategy. Typically, noticeable improvements in rankings and traffic occur within 3 to 6 months, depending on your industry and competition.
What is technical SEO?
Technical SEO involves optimizing your website's infrastructure, such as site speed, mobile-friendliness, XML sitemaps, and Core Web Vitals, to help search engines crawl and index your site effectively.
Do you provide Local or National SEO?
We provide both. Whether you are a local Bradford business targeting nearby customers or an e-commerce brand seeking national visibility across the UK, we tailor our strategy accordingly.
Are your SEO strategies safe?
Yes, we use 100% white-hat SEO techniques that strictly follow Google's Webmaster Guidelines, ensuring long-term, sustainable growth without the risk of penalties.
Do I get monthly reports?
Absolutely. We provide transparent, easy-to-understand monthly performance reports detailing keyword rankings, traffic growth, and work completed.
