AI Development FAQs

What is a custom AI agent?
Unlike basic chatbots, a custom AI agent is powered by large language models (like GPT-4) connected directly to your business databases. It understands your context and can execute real tasks autonomously.
Can AI replace my staff?
No, our AI solutions are designed to augment your team, not replace them. We automate repetitive administrative tasks so your staff can focus on high-value, creative, and strategic work.
How is data kept secure?
We build enterprise-grade pipelines using secure vector databases and API configurations with zero-data-retention policies, meaning your data is never used to train public models.
How long does a build take?
Simple AI integrations can be deployed in 2-3 weeks. Complex, custom-trained AI agents deeply integrated into your ERP or CRM typically take 6-10 weeks.
Can it integrate with my CRM?
Yes, we specialize in building intelligent middleware that securely connects modern LLMs with legacy systems, CRMs, and ERP platforms.
