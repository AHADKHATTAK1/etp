The Hidden Cost of Bad PPC

Most UK businesses running Google Ads are either wasting budget on broad match keywords that don't convert, or running campaigns with no negative keyword lists. It’s incredibly common to see well-intentioned owners pouring money into Google, only to find that their ads are showing up for completely irrelevant search terms. The average small business loses 40–60% of their Google Ads budget to irrelevant clicks. That's real money that could have been used to generate actual leads, effectively being flushed down the drain every single month.

We see the same mistakes repeatedly: relying on default Google recommendations designed to increase ad spend, rather than tailoring campaigns to strict match types and negative lists. Without proper tracking, businesses can't even tell which clicks led to sales and which didn't. This creates a cycle of frustration and wasted resources. It's time to stop paying for clicks and start paying for customers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Google Ads management cost in the UK?

Our management fees start at £299/month for starter campaigns (up to £1,000 ad spend), scaling to £799/month for enterprise accounts. Ad spend is billed separately by Google.

What is the minimum Google Ads budget I need?

While there is no strict minimum, we generally recommend starting with at least £500 to £1,000 per month in ad spend to generate meaningful data and results.

How quickly will I see results from Google Ads?

Google Ads can drive traffic immediately upon launch. However, it typically takes 2-4 weeks to gather enough data to fully optimise campaigns for the best cost-per-lead and ROI.

Do you manage Shopping ads for e-commerce?

Yes, we handle full Google Merchant Center setup, feed optimisation, and standard/Performance Max Shopping campaigns for e-commerce brands.

What makes ETechProvider different from other Google Ads agencies?
