Shopify FAQs

Shopify vs WooCommerce: Which is better?
For most e-commerce businesses, Shopify offers superior reliability, security, and a better checkout experience. WooCommerce is viable if you require deep integration with an existing complex WordPress content site.
Do you migrate stores to Shopify?
Yes, we handle seamless data migrations from Magento, WooCommerce, Wix, and custom platforms, ensuring your SEO, products, and customer data remain intact.
What is headless Shopify?
Headless Shopify separates the front-end (often built in Next.js or Hydrogen) from the Shopify backend. It provides ultra-fast loading speeds and limitless design flexibility for high-volume brands.
How long does a custom Shopify build take?
A standard custom theme build takes roughly 4-6 weeks. Complex Shopify Plus setups or headless architectures can take 8-12 weeks.
Do you offer ongoing support after launch?
Absolutely. E-commerce is continuous. We provide ongoing support, conversion rate optimization (CRO) iterations, and new app integrations as your store grows.
